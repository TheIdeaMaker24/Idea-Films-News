Overview

Idea Films serves as the production banner for multiple film, television-style, and online projects.

Several productions exist within shared fictional continuities, including the Earth-500 LEGO Marvel YouTube Universe.

History

Idea Films began as a creative outlet for independently produced videos and gradually expanded into larger narrative projects, reaction content, and interconnected film series.

Over time, the label expanded its production style to include stop-motion animation, visual effects, voice acting, original music, and serialized storytelling.

Series and universes

Earth-500

Earth-500 is an interconnected LEGO Marvel cinematic universe featuring characters including Spider-Man, the X-Men, Fantastic Four, Guardians of the Galaxy, and others.

The universe is designed around multiple phases of interconnected films, specials, and crossover events.

Special projects

Idea Films may also produce standalone films, miniseries, crossover specials, reaction projects, and experimental productions.
